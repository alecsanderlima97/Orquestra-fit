# Third-party assets

## Human anatomy base model

`public/models/human-anatomy-base.glb` is the `human_posed.glb` asset from
[UMRAM-Bilkent/supine-human-model](https://github.com/UMRAM-Bilkent/supine-human-model).
It is derived from a Quaternius character and released under the CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication.
